# Operations Lifecycle

The Operations Lifecycle is a framework for managing operational activities across three interconnected phases. Each phase has a distinct purpose and supporting capabilities.

---

## Overview

Operations management follows a continuous cycle of planning, execution, and optimization. This lifecycle ensures that resources are used efficiently, operations run smoothly, and improvements are continuously identified and implemented.

---

## Phase 1: Plan

**Purpose:** *Save money before it is spent*

The planning phase focuses on upfront design, simulation, and optimization to prevent costly mistakes during execution.

### Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Planning, Layout & Simulation** | Design operational layouts and simulate processes before implementation |
| **Capacity & Schedule Optimization** | Balance resources, demand, and timing for maximum efficiency |
| **Management [ERP/MRP]** | Enterprise Resource Planning and Material Requirements Planning systems to coordinate resources |

---

## Phase 2: Run & Monitor

**Purpose:** *Manage by leading insight vs. lagging indicators*

The execution phase focuses on real-time visibility and proactive management rather than reactive responses to problems.

### Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **IoT & Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE)** | Sensor-driven data collection and equipment performance measurement |
| **Real-time Location Services** | Track assets, materials, and personnel in real-time |
| **Work Instructions & Execution Systems** | Standardized procedures and MES (Manufacturing Execution Systems) |
| **Environmental Health & Safety** | Monitor and ensure compliance with EHS requirements |

---

## Phase 3: Maintain & Optimize

**Purpose:** *Protect mission-critical value every day*

The optimization phase focuses on preserving asset value and continuously improving operations.

### Key Capabilities

| Capability | Description |
|------------|-------------|
| **Asset & Maintenance Management (CMMS/EAM)** | Computerized Maintenance Management Systems and Enterprise Asset Management |
| **Condition Monitoring & Predictive Maintenance** | Use data to predict failures before they occur |
| **Quality & Execution Guidance** | Ensure consistent quality through guided processes |

---

## Key Principles

1. **Continuous Cycle:** The three phases feed into each other—insights from Maintain & Optimize inform future Planning
2. **Leading vs. Lagging Indicators:** Focus on predictive metrics rather than historical ones
3. **Proactive over Reactive:** Prevent problems rather than fix them
4. **Data-Driven Decisions:** Use IoT, sensors, and analytics across all phases

---

## Application

This framework applies to:
- Manufacturing operations
- Facility management
- Supply chain operations
- Asset-intensive industries
- Service operations with physical components
